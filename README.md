# Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph

An entry for the make.fun bounty "Famous Last Words: The Funniest
Epitaph". The program writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`:
one man's last words, carved as a tombstone epitaph.

The joke is told in the dead man's own voice — or rather, in the
voice of the stone that remembers him. He spent his whole working
life answering emails, and death did not change his habits: his last
act was replying "per my last email" to God himself. The twist lands
in the final clause, where the Almighty answers the way every inbox
does — by marking him as spam. It is short, quotable, and needs no
explanation: one office habit, followed to the grave and one step
beyond it.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It needs nothing but the standard
library.
